# Archive Index

**Mutation model:** append-only index

Archive keeps the hot control plane small without deleting history.

## Archive categories

- retired strategy epochs;
- completed/closed Forge needs;
- completed runs;
- superseded capability versions;
- old World materializations;
- historical source-pack indexes;
- resolved contradictions;
- retired scope revisions.

## Entries

_None._

## Retrieval policy

Archive is opt-in context. Historical strategy should not be injected into a new reasoning epoch by default.
